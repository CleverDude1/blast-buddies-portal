Blast Buddies Companion

A small browser extension that follows your grind and your clan rivalry while you play, so you don’t have to keep checking stats pages.

Download for Chrome and Edge

Version 0.1.0. Free and unofficial. It works on Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers.

What it does

  • Hourly gains. Your kills over the last hour, on the game screen and on the toolbar icon.
  • Rival clan comparison. Your clan against your rival per hour, with the kill gap between you.
  • Red outline warning. The game window gets a red border when your rival is close to passing you. You choose how close.
  • Overtake alerts. A notification when the rival closes in, passes you, or when you take the lead back.
  • While you were away. When you come back, a popup shows what your clan and the rival gained while you were gone.
  • Busiest hours. Tells you when the game is usually busiest, like “busiest in 3h”.
  • Session summary. When you stop playing, you see how long you played and how many kills you got.
  • Your choice. Every feature has an on/off switch and its own numbers in the extension dashboard.

Install it

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You get a folder called blast-buddies-companion.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, or edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin the extension from the puzzle icon in the toolbar, so you can see its badge.

Set it up

  1. Click the extension icon. The dashboard opens.
  2. Find your IDs with the search below, then paste them into Your clan ID, Rival clan ID and Your player ID.
  3. Switch features on or off and set your numbers, like how close the rival must be before the red outline shows.
  4. Open the game. Your numbers appear in the top right corner.

Type a player name and copy the IDs. For a rival clan’s ID, search any member of that clan.

Good to know

  • The tracker updates about once an hour, so your numbers, the gap and the red outline refresh about hourly too.
  • It only reads public stats from this site. Your settings stay on your device, and it never asks for your login.
  • Chrome may show a “disable developer mode extensions” message when it starts. Dismiss it and keep the extension.
  • To update, download the new zip, replace the old folder, and click the refresh icon on the extension’s card in chrome://extensions.
